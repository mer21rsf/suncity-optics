Upcoming requests

Booking requests

Each request reserves its full service duration. Requested, Confirmed and Completed visits block their interval. Cancelling or an admin deleting a visit releases the slot. Contact the customer before confirming; status changes here do not email them.

Export CSV ↓Open Setmore ↗
Setmore bookings are managed separately. This dashboard contains requests submitted on this website; it does not automatically import or synchronize Setmore appointments.

Block unavailable time

Reserve a lunch break, staff absence or closure. One shared shop calendar is used for all services. Blocks that overlap existing bookings are refused; review those bookings first.

Make the homepage your own

Edit the built-in design here. Changes are saved when you choose Save website details.

JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF; maximum 8 MB. Changes the “Made for your point of view” area, one featured image at a time. Uploading saves the photo immediately. The built-in homepage uses this photo; edit images in Word for a Word-designed homepage.

Use an image you own or have permission to use. The image URL must be publicly accessible.

Opening hours

Enter 24-hour times, for example 09:00–17:00, or Closed. All times use America/Vancouver.

Services

Design it in Word. Share it on the web.

Import a .docx document to replace the main content of your homepage. The website header, appointment form, contact section, chatbox and footer remain available.

Supported: text, headings, bold/italic/underline, text colours, alignment, simple tables, clickable HTTPS/HTTP/email/phone links, and embedded PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP images. Complex positioning, floating text boxes, fonts, shapes, headers and footers may change or be omitted. Images should be inserted In Line with Text. Keep important text outside images. Use a normal Word hyperlink to #booking for your booking button.

Importing does not change the live page until you publish. Switching back preserves the Word draft. Linked pages must already exist online; Word does not upload the target of a link.

Sun City Stories — review the note wall

Only Approved notes are public. Email addresses remain private. Review each note for personal or medical information before publishing. Staff accounts cannot approve, reject or delete these notes.

Create a staff login

Give each person their own account so changes can be attributed to them. Share the temporary password privately.

At least 12 characters. Required for new accounts; leave blank when editing to keep the existing password. A reset requires a password change at next sign-in.

Individual sign-ins

Disable accounts to remove access while preserving their change history. Do not share accounts if you need to identify each employee.

Who changed what

Recent 500 activity entries, newest first. Times use Surrey local time. Logs include account activity and changes to settings, bookings, employee profiles and page publication. Passwords are never included. These are application logs, not an independently tamper-proof audit service.

Admin only. Cleanup removes the chosen entries and keeps one new receipt recording who performed the cleanup. Deleted entries cannot be recovered from this page.

Booking & message alerts

Choose up to three addresses to receive new request and message alerts. Customer details remain in this dashboard.

SMTP or HTTPS email credentials are configured on the server. Email addresses alone do not enable delivery. Alerts retry on a one-minute schedule when installed.

Change admin password

At least 12 characters. Changing your password signs out your own sessions. Reset a forgotten password using the setup command on your server.
