Opening hours

Services

Design it in Word. Share it on the web.

Import a .docx document to replace the main content of your homepage. The website header, appointment form, contact section, chatbox and footer remain available.

Supported: text, headings, bold/italic/underline, text colours, alignment, simple tables, clickable HTTPS/HTTP/email/phone links, and embedded PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP images. Complex positioning, floating text boxes, fonts, shapes, headers and footers may change or be omitted. Images should be inserted In Line with Text. Keep important text outside images. Use a normal Word hyperlink to #booking for your booking button.

Importing does not change the live page until you publish. Switching back preserves the Word draft. Linked pages must already exist online; Word does not upload the target of a link.

Your team

Messages from your chatbox

Visitors can leave messages when your team is away. Reply using your email app. This inbox is not a live chat service.

Booking & message alerts

Choose up to three addresses to receive new request and message alerts. Customer details remain in this dashboard.

SMTP sending credentials are configured on the server. Email addresses alone do not enable delivery. Alerts retry on a one-minute schedule when installed.

Change admin password

At least 12 characters. Changing the password signs out all sessions. Reset a forgotten password using the setup command on your server.
